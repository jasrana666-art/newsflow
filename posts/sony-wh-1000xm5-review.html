Sony WH-1000XM5 headphones reviewed

The best noise-cancelling headphones in 2026 — but at $298, are they worth it? We tested them for 30 days.

01 — WEIGHT & COMFORT

Ears have spoken

We wore the WH-1000XM5 for 6–8 hours a day across 30 days. The first thing you notice is the weight — 240g is noticeably lighter than the XM4 (250g) and about the same as the Bose QC45. After a week, the ears adapt. After a month, the foam cups are still comfortable. The memory foam earpads are plush, slightly domed — good for glasses wearers. The headband sits without digging. Over 30 days, there was zero fatigue on long sessions.

02 — ACTIVE NOISE CANCELLATION

The king of quiet

Sony's 360 Audio Sense mic array and AI-driven ANC noise detection is the best in class. We walked past construction zones, rode the subway, sat in coffee shops. The XM5 consistently pulled in the lowest background-noise readings. Low-end rumble from traffic is almost completely gone. High-frequency chatter is suppressed but not eliminated — which is right, because you still hear important sounds like alarms, or a train announcement that says "your stop coming up."

03 — SOUND QUALITY

Very good, not amazing

The XM5 has good tuning — bright, detailed, slightly bass-light with a fair amount of treble. V-shaped at the treble end. The soundstage is wide and clear. But it's not as comfortable as the Bose QC45 for bass-heavy music, and the lack of a dedicated "vocal" mode means vocal tracks can sound a touch distant. For most listeners, this is a non-issue — the XM5 sounds excellent in everything except heavy sub-bass.

04 — BATTERY LIFE

24 hours is generous

Sony claims 30 hours with ANC on and Adaptive Sound Control turned off. In our testing, we got 27–29 hours of real-world use. That's still outstanding — 62% of the way through 100% of the battery with 40 hours of daily commute. Bluetooth 5.3 with low-latency mode is excellent for podcasts and video calls. The USB-C charging port delivers 3.5 hours for 6 hours of playback.

05 — FEATURES & ACCESSORIES

Smart, but not smartest

The XM5 comes with a carrying case, an adapter (2.4GHz or USB-C), and a 3.5mm cable. The adaptive sound control is good — it detects your environment and adjusts ANC automatically. The Bluetooth pairing is effortless, and the multitouch buttons on the earcups are responsive. The touch sensitivity is adjustable in the app, so you can make it more or less sensitive.

06 — PROS & CONS

✅ Pros

  • • Best noise cancellation in the class
  • • Comfortable for all-day wear
  • • Touch controls + app support
  • • 240g — light for over-ear
  • • Multipoint Bluetooth

❌ Cons

  • • Bass is not as deep as Bose QC45
  • • EQ could be better

VERDICT

Who should buy this

The Sony WH-1000XM5 is the best noise-cancelling headphone for most people. If you commute on the subway, work in a noisy office, or just want a pair of headphones that make any environment feel like a library, this is the one. At $298, it's 20% more than the Bose QC45, and you get a better noise cancellation in exchange for slightly less bass and a smaller soundstage.

Buy it if: You prioritize noise cancellation, want all-day comfort, and listen to podcasts or watch video.

Skip it if: You need serious sub-bass for EDM or hip-hop, or you prefer open-back headphones.
