Notion AI Workspace reviewed

The smart workspace that writes for you. We tested Notion AI for 30 days — here's what it actually does well (and where it falls short).

Notion AI Workspace
The smartest all-in-one workspace. Not the smartest writer.
01 — WHAT NOTION AI DOES WELL

Writing assistant for the whole team

Notion AI is embedded in the workspace — no separate login, no separate app. You can ask AI to: summarise a long database, write a meeting summary, generate a project brief, draft an email, or even write a blog post from a few bullet points. We tested this across every task, and the results were consistently good. The summarisation is particularly strong — when you have 20 pages of meeting notes, Notion AI compresses them into a 5-bullet summary that captures the key decisions.

02 — WHAT IT CAN'T DO

Writing assistant, not a writer

The AI writes — but it doesn't edit. The output is grammatically correct, but the voice is flat. It doesn't match your brand's tone. It doesn't understand your audience. If you're writing a client proposal or a marketing email, you'll need to rewrite it in your own voice. The AI is a good first draft tool, but not a final document.

03 — DATABASE & AUTOMATION

The real power

Notion's real strength isn't the AI — it's the workspace. We built a project tracker, a meeting log, a knowledge base, and a client database. The AI connected to all of these. You can ask: "What's the status of the launch project?" and it pulls from the database, summarises, and gives you a clear answer. The integration between pages is seamless.

04 — PRICING

$10/month is the deal

The AI add-on costs $10/month per user. That unlocks 200 AI credits per month. We used about 150 credits in a month of normal usage. The base Notion plan is $10/month for 1-5 users. Together, that's $20/month. The AI credits are plenty for a small team, and free credits are generous.

PROS & CONS

✅ Pros

  • • Embedded AI in the workspace
  • • 200 free credits/month
  • • Great summarisation
  • • Works with all Notion databases
  • • Real-time collaboration

❌ Cons

  • • Flat writing voice
  • • No brand voice automation
  • • Not great for technical docs
  • • Requires manual editing
  • • Overwhelming at first
VERDICT

Who should use Notion AI

Notion AI is the smartest workspace for teams who live in Notion. It's not a writer — it's a first-draft generator and a summariser. If you're a solo creator, you might find it faster to just write. If you're on a team managing documentation, projects, or knowledge, the AI write-ups save hours every week.
